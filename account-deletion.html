Elimina definitivamente il tuo account collegato a ShareATail (ex CatWell) e tutti i dati associati (profili gatti e cani, vaccini, visite, diario, storie e backup cloud) direttamente dall'app oppure via email.

ShareATail Account & Data Deletion

Permanently delete your linked ShareATail (formerly CatWell) account and all associated data (cat & dog profiles, vaccines, vet visits, diary, stories, and cloud backups) directly inside the app or by email.

App: ShareATail (com.gabrielo.catwell) Developer: Gabriele O. Contact: devapp.gabrielo@gmail.com

Option 1 — Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open ShareATail on your Android phone.
  2. Tap Settings (gear icon in the top-right corner).
  3. Scroll to Privacy & Data and tap Delete account and all data.
  4. Confirm deletion (if your Google sign-in session is old, ShareATail will ask for a quick security re-login).
Warning: Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Email Request (If You Already Uninstalled the App)

Email devapp.gabrielo@gmail.com from the same Google email address used in ShareATail (typically completed within 7 business days, maximum 30 days).
